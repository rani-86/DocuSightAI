## Retake validation on the controlled calibration images

Decision made by the calibrated gate on each calibration image that has an OCR result (`scripts/quality_vs_cer.py`). 'Raw CER' = OCR of the unprocessed image; 'best-variant CER' = the lower of raw and enhanced (what the best available fix achieves). These are the same images the thresholds were derived from, so this checks the *consistency* of the gate with the experiment, not generalisation (generalisation is the data/test table above).

| enhance | 11 | 0.0837 | 0.0650 | 18% |
